Understanding D.Core models

The names searched most are the D.Core 2, the D.Core Cirrus (including Cirrus JP and Cirrus II) and the D.Core Stratus (including Stratus JP). We list them as recognition aids, not reviews. Roller behavior, body-scan and intensity options, recline style and price differ between them. “JP” and revised names can signal a different generation or market version, so ask the seller to put the exact model and revision in writing and confirm it is on the floor before you visit.

Reading D.Core’s wellness and feature claims

Terms like “4D” or “recovery” are prompts to verify. If a model advertises 4D massage, test whether the added control feels better for your body after 10–15 minutes.

Reviews and showroom testing can help buyers evaluate comfort, but they should not be treated as medical evidence. Buyers with diagnosed conditions should consult a healthcare professional. Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy authentic D.Core massage chairs?

▾

Buy from a seller authorized to support the brand, and confirm the unit is a genuine, current model. Ask for the exact model and revision in writing, and check that the warranty is valid through that seller.

How does the D.Core 2 compare to other models?

▾

The D.Core 2, Cirrus and Stratus differ in roller behavior, intensity options, recline, feel and price. Sit in each model, run a full program, and judge comfort and body fit rather than styling.

How do I avoid buying an outdated or fake D.Core chair?

▾

Get the model name and revision in writing, confirm it is a current unit, and verify that the seller is authorized. Be wary of unusually steep discounts or vague model names.

Should I try D.Core before buying?

▾

Yes, trying the exact model in person is the most reliable test. Design and reputation can’t tell you whether a chair fits your frame or pressure preference.

What warranty does D.Core list on its official site?

▾

As read on 22 Oct 2026, D.Core’s official warranty page lists a 5-year limited warranty on the D.Core 2 and a 3-year limited warranty on the Cirrus-JP and Stratus-JP, with repair or replacement at the company’s discretion and on-site service as necessary. It applies to the original purchaser for residential use and requires a surge protector. Confirm the terms for your exact model before buying.
