What OHCO massage chairs are generally known for

Reading OHCO’s wellness and feature claims

Premium chairs are often described with words like intelligent body scanning or recovery, so ask what a named feature does for your comfort and judge it by feel rather than vocabulary.

Note

Be cautious with any chair described as “medical-grade,” “clinically proven,” “doctor recommended,” “FDA approved,” or claimed to “improve circulation,” “relieve sciatica” or treat any condition — verify exactly what such phrases mean and who is making them. Reviews and showroom testing can help buyers evaluate comfort, but they should not be treated as medical evidence. Buyers with diagnosed conditions should consult a healthcare professional.

Frequently asked questions

Are OHCO massage chairs worth the premium price?

▾

The answer depends on your body more than the badge: the finish is premium, but a chair that doesn’t fit your frame or pressure preference isn’t worth it at any price, so test the exact model and confirm warranty, service and delivery first.

What should I know before buying an OHCO M8 or M8 NEO?

▾

Sit in the exact model for several minutes and check the footprint and reclined clearance, then get delivery, white-glove installation, warranty coverage and return terms in writing.

How does the OHCO M8 NEO differ from the original M8?

▾

A “NEO” label usually signals updates to massage feel, programs, scan or finish, so treat it as a prompt to test: sit in both if you can and judge comfort after ten to fifteen minutes.

Should I try an OHCO chair in person before buying?

▾

Yes, an unhurried in-person test is the most reliable protection against an expensive mistake at this price, so run a full program, check the body scan and compare another model the same day.

Where can I buy an OHCO massage chair, online or in a showroom?

▾

OHCO chairs are sold online and through showrooms, and for a chair this size many buyers test in person first. If you buy online, verify return terms and how warranty repairs work.

What warranty does OHCO list on its official site?

▾

As read on 22 Oct 2026, OHCO’s official warranty page describes a residential 3-year limited repair-or-replacement warranty with on-site service on regular workdays. It applies to the original purchaser with dated proof of purchase, does not require registration, and requires a surge protector of at least 2,000 joules. Confirm the current terms for your model.
