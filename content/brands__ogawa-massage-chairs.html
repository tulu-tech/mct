What Ogawa massage chairs are generally known for

Ogawa draws attention for technology-forward features: body scanning, software-driven programs and language around AI and diagnostic-style sensing. The lineup spans feature-heavy flagships and simpler, more affordable chairs.

Understanding Ogawa models

Frequently searched names include the Ogawa Master Drive Duo, Master Drive Duo LE, Master Drive AI 2.0, Active XL 3D, Active L 3D and Refresh L. We name them as recognition aids only. Ogawa presents the Master Drive line as the more feature-rich, AI-focused tier, and the Active and Refresh lines as more accessible. Roller systems, body scan, recline, frame size and price differ, so check that the exact model is on the floor before you visit.

Questions to ask before buying Ogawa

  • Who issues the warranty for a chair bought in the US, and what are its parts, labor and frame terms?
  • Who performs in-home service in the US, and in which areas?
  • Is this a US-market model with US support, rather than an imported unit?
  • What is the warranty period, and does it start on the date of purchase?
  • Will I need the serial number, warranty card or dated proof of purchase as the original purchaser?

Reading Ogawa's wellness and feature claims

“AI,” “diagnostic” or “recovery” can mean different things by model, or little at all. Ask what a feature does for your body, not what it is called.

Note

Reviews and showroom testing can help buyers evaluate comfort, but they should not be treated as medical evidence. Buyers with diagnosed conditions should consult a healthcare professional. A massage chair is a comfort and relaxation product that may offer temporary relief of minor muscle tension for some people; it is not a medical device.
