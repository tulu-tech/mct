What Bodyfriend massage chairs are generally known for

Bodyfriend competes on luxury-tech features, premium materials and design-forward cabinets. Shoppers often consider a Bodyfriend massage chair for standout features such as the Falcon’s Rovo walking technology, a stepping-style lower-body motion.

Understanding Bodyfriend models

Names in searches include the Bodyfriend Falcon and Falcon SV, the Phantom and the Pharaoh, and the Falcon line is the one most associated with walking-style movement. Bodyfriend’s Falcon page lists features such as ROVO Walking Technology for individual leg stretching, Zero Gravity Recline, a foot roller massage, safety sensors and a Bluetooth speaker. Test the Falcon massage chair in person before you rely on that list.

Reading Bodyfriend’s wellness and feature claims

Terms like “walking technology,” “4D” or “recovery” don’t tell you how a chair will feel, so test each one in the seat.

Note

Reviews and showroom testing can help buyers evaluate comfort, but they should not be treated as medical evidence. Buyers with diagnosed conditions should consult a healthcare professional. A massage chair is a comfort and relaxation product that may offer temporary relief of minor muscle tension for some people; it is not a treatment.

Frequently asked questions

What should I know before buying the Bodyfriend Falcon?

▾

The Falcon is the model most associated with walking-style movement, so test that motion by feel. Sit in it for several minutes, check the foot and calf sections, and compare it with a simpler chair.

What warranty does Bodyfriend list on its official site?

▾

As read on 22 Oct 2026, Bodyfriend’s official warranty page lists 3 years for the Falcon and 1 year for the Familie, Eliza and Onew, with repair or replacement at Bodyfriend’s option for the original purchaser within the continental US. It says the purchaser is responsible for returning the product, so ask how that works for a heavy chair before you buy.
